What to Expect During Your EPC Assessment

Booking an Energy Performance Certificate doesn't need to be complicated. Here's what to expect.

01

Book Your Appointment

Get in touch to arrange a convenient time for your domestic EPC assessment.

02

Your Property Assessment

Nicole will assess key features of your property, including heating, insulation, windows, lighting and construction.

03

How to Prepare

Please ensure safe access to heating controls and the loft hatch, where applicable. Any relevant documentation about insulation or improvements may also be helpful.

04

Receive Your Certificate

Your EPC will be lodged on the official register. Same-day lodgement is available where possible.
