How it works

An EPC assessment, made simple.

Your assessment is a straightforward visit to review the energy-related features of your property.

  1. 01

    Get in touch

    Arrange a convenient appointment with Nicole.

  2. 02

    Your property assessment

    Nicole checks relevant features including windows, heating, insulation, lighting and construction.

  3. 03

    Prepare for the visit

    Where applicable, allow safe access to heating controls and the loft hatch. Information about improvements can help.

  4. 04

    Receive your certificate

    Your EPC is lodged on the official register. Same-day lodgement is available where possible.
